# Changelog

## 2.1.0

First public release.

### Paulstretch

- Extreme time-stretching of a recorded or loaded sound, up to 8192 times its length, at the same pitch.
- Records live stereo input, or loads a WAV or AIFF file. A loaded file can optionally be stored inside the patch.
- STRETCH, WINDOW, PITCH and START controls, with CV for stretch and pitch (V/OCT).
- FREEZE holds one moment as a drone.
- REVERSE reads backwards.
- SCAN moves the read position by CV.

### Paulstretch Heads

- Expander for Paulstretch: four more playback heads on the same sound.
- Each head has its own stretch, pitch, start, freeze, reverse, scan and stereo outputs.
- Up to three expanders can be chained, for twelve heads in total.
